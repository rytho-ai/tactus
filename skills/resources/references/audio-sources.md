# Downloadable audio sources

Use this when selecting audio to acquire. The two recommendation tables contain **23 sources** with
specific zero-payment downloads checked on **2026-10-15**, without accounts, email
submission or paid trials. Every audio file in the listed downloaded packs, and each
listed individual file, passed full decoding; the scope is **6,142 audio-file entries**,
including **4,358 single-cycle synthesis waveforms**. One bundled demo was also decoded
but is not counted as usable sample material. These are file entries, not a claim of
unique sounds or thousands of ready-made music loops (AKWF includes four duplicate pairs).
Recheck
the selected item's current page and license. This is technical verification, not a
listening evaluation or a guarantee about other files on the same service. Sources that
failed or did not deliver original audio are separated below.

For Splice-like browsing by BPM, key and instrument, see
[searchable loop libraries](#searchable-loop-libraries-download-and-use-status).
For API-based sound-effect discovery, see [Openverse](#openverse-audio-search-api).

## Openverse audio search API

[Openverse](https://api.openverse.org/v1/) aggregates audio from other hosts. Use it
to discover sound effects; download and verify the selected original from its source.
This entry was checked **2026-10-16** and is outside the 23-source totals above.

- **Search:** anonymous `GET https://api.openverse.org/v1/audio/?q=door&license=cc0`
  works without an account or payment. Replace `q` with the needed sound; add
  `source=wikimedia_audio` to narrow to Wikimedia. `category=sound_effect` is supported,
  but relevant effects can have `category=null`; retry without that filter if results
  are sparse. See the [API schema](https://api.openverse.org/v1/schema/) for parameters.
- **Select and acquire:** follow `foreign_landing_url` to check the creator and current
  license, then identify the offered original download. Inspect `url` and `alt_files`:
  `url` can be a compressed preview. In the tested Freesound result, it was an MP3
  preview; the original download returned HTTP 401. Original Freesound downloads need
  [site login](https://freesound.org/help/faq/#how-do-i-download-a-sound) or
  [Freesound OAuth2](https://freesound.org/docs/api/resources_apiv2.html#download-sound-oauth2-required).
  Openverse authentication does not grant access to another host.
- **Verified original:** [Door knocker audio — Mx. Granger](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Door_knocker_audio.ogg),
  source page CC0 1.0; [original OGG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7c/Door_knocker_audio.ogg)
  downloaded anonymously, 30,423 bytes, 44.1 kHz mono, 65,000 frames / 1.474 s.
  SoundFile full read produced finite samples; strict full FFmpeg decoding exited zero
  with no errors. SHA-256:
  `3362b0bd105382bd8f5e0d268b8e7318233abd966c43449241bfee1f35b47c35`.
  This verifies one original file, not every result or its musical suitability.
- **API operation:** read response rate-limit headers and respect HTTP 429. Anonymous
  responses in this check advertised 20/min and 200/day; recheck rather than hardcode
  these as guaranteed quotas. [Authentication and throttling](https://docs.openverse.org/api/reference/authentication_and_throttling.html)
  describes higher-limit access. The [terms](https://docs.openverse.org/terms_of_service.html)
  require source-license compliance and, for an API-powered application, visible
  Openverse attribution without implying endorsement. Openverse metadata is a lead;
  preserve the selected source's license and attribution with the acquired file.

## Personal Splice-like projects: separate from verified audio

- [SeaWav](https://seawav.org/oneshot): personal sample-map browser; search and playback
  worked, but the tested save shortcut did not produce an original file. For selected
  `347166__dvbeats__kick-drum-1.wav`, playback fetched a compressed `_cmp.m4a` preview.
  The [original DVBeats item](https://freesound.org/people/DVBeats/sounds/347166/) is CC0;
  that verifies this item's rights, not SeaWav's original-download path or whole catalog.
- [Sonura](https://app.sonurastudio.com/samples): community library exists, with Free Sounds,
  search and download buttons. Tested download requires sign-in; no original audio acquired.
  Its [terms §4.2](https://sonurastudio.com/terms/) dedicate publicly shared samples to CC0.
  Recheck account conditions; generation credits do not establish community download limits.
- [Bangerly](https://bangerly.com/): community samples and packs; [help](https://bangerly.com/help)
  describes five weekly free credits. The free Lo-fi pack routes to sign-in; the tested
  single-sample download routes to `/signin` and a 404. No original audio acquired.
- [Splicedd](https://github.com/ascpixi/splicedd) and [Splicerr](https://github.com/Robert-K/splicerr):
  third-party Splice frontends. Their preview-to-WAV path does not establish original,
  licensed sample acquisition; [Splice terms](https://splice.com/terms) restrict previews to
  auditioning in the service. Do not recommend them as verified free audio sources.
- [OpenLoop](https://github.com/moddroid94/openloop): self-hosted management of the user's
  existing samples, not a supplied public audio library.

Account-gated sites may still offer free audio, but remain unverified until an authorized
download succeeds. Keep those results separate instead of filling the deliverable with
landing-page links.
